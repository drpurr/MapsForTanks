# Credits

## Route drawings
Routes are adapted from the [MapsForTanks](https://mapsfortanks.wordpress.com/) guides (2011–12).
The original images are in `maps-src/<Zone>/<Dungeon>/`.

## Map art
- **Game maps** (most dungeons and raids) come from the WoW Forever client's own world-map art.
  Map art © Blizzard Entertainment.
- **WoW Forever–only instances** use maps by **Santiago Reyes**, from *Atlas of Azeroth: Forever*
  (https://www.artstation.com/artwork/lEG15k). They are used with the artist's permission,
  which the project owner obtained on 2026-11-19. The originals are in `maps-src/external/atlas-of-azeroth-forever/`.
  They were sourced from the artist's maps as published on MMONerds guide pages. The site shows the credit
  under every map that uses this art.
  - Alcaz Prison (main prison, Maximum Security Unit)
  - Blackmaw Hold
  - City of Dalaran (city, sewers)
  - Excavation Site: Wetlands
  - Krol'dok Stronghold
  - Ruins of Lordaeron
  - The Drowned City
  - The Hall of Thanes
